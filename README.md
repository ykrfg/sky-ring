SKY RING is an entry for the make.fun ["Make the best flight game"](https://make.fun/bounty/best-flight-game-20261126-0057) bounty.

An original, stylized flat-shaded 3D flight game that plays in a desktop browser, fully offline from its own files.

Take off from the island runway, fly the numbered target-ring course over the sea and coastal towns, then bring the plane back for a landing. The HUD shows altitude, speed and heading with a lit cockpit panel and an attitude indicator.

- **Controls**: ↑/↓ pitch, ←/→ bank (bank to turn), W/S throttle, gentle and slow on final approach to land
- **Combat**: enemy aircraft fly set routes over the island — hold your aim on one to lock on, then fire a missile with **J** or **F** for a score bonus; **G** raises and lowers the landing gear
- **Play locally**: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080
- **Entry point for grading**: `node main.ts` writes the playable game, screenshots and `dossier.json` to the output directory

様式化されたフラットシェーディング3Dのフライトゲームです。島の滑走路から離陸し、海上のターゲットリングを順にくぐって、最後に滑走路へ着陸します。ブラウザでオフライン動作します。
